Q.6 Dynamically Equivalent 2-Mass System Derivation (Summer-2025 Q.3b - 6 Marks)[span_74](start_span)[span_74](end_span)
Question: State the conditions under which a body is replaced by a dynamically equivalent system of two masses. Derive the relation l₁ ⋅ l₂ = k².[span_75](start_span)[span_75](end_span)[span_76](start_span)[span_76](end_span)[span_77](start_span)[span_77](end_span)
Conditions: (1) $m = m_1 + m_2$, (2) $m_1 \cdot l_1 = m_2 \cdot l_2$, (3) $I = m_1 l_1^2 + m_2 l_2^2 = m \cdot k^2$.
From condition (2): $m_2 = m_1 \frac{l_1}{l_2}$. Substituting into (1):
$$m_1 \left[ 1 + \frac{l_1}{l_2} \right] = m \implies m_1 = m \left[ \frac{l_2}{l_1 + l_2} \right], \quad m_2 = m \left[ \frac{l_1}{l_1 + l_2} \right]$$
Substituting $m_1$ and $m_2$ into moment of inertia condition (3):
$$m \left[ \frac{l_2}{l_1 + l_2} \right] l_1^2 + m \left[ \frac{l_1}{l_1 + l_2} \right] l_2^2 = m \cdot k^2$$
$$\frac{m \cdot l_1 l_2 (l_1 + l_2)}{l_1 + l_2} = m \cdot k^2 \implies l_1 \cdot l_2 = k^2$$
Derived Condition: $l_1 \cdot l_2 = k^2$

Q.4 Balancing Theory: Why Partial Balancing (Summer-2025 Q.11a - 4 Marks)[span_235](start_span)[span_235](end_span)
Question: Why is only a part of the unbalanced force due to reciprocating masses balanced in locomotives? State the condition to prevent hammer blow from lifting wheels.[span_236](start_span)[span_236](end_span)[span_237](start_span)[span_237](end_span)[span_238](start_span)[span_238](end_span)
1. Reason for Partial Balancing: Reciprocating forces act along the horizontal line of stroke ($m \omega^2 r \cos \theta$). To balance them, revolving counter-masses are added to the driving wheels. While these balance horizontal forces, their vertical component ($m \omega^2 r \sin \theta$) remains completely unbalanced, creating alternating upward and downward vertical forces (Hammer Blow)[span_239](start_span)[span_239](end_span)[span_240](start_span)[span_240](end_span)[span_241](start_span)[span_241](end_span). If 100% of reciprocating mass were balanced, the vertical impact on the track and wheel lifting would be severe. Therefore, only 50% to 75% ($c = 2/3$) is balanced[span_242](start_span)[span_242](end_span)[span_243](start_span)[span_243](end_span)[span_244](start_span)[span_244](end_span).
2. Condition to Prevent Wheel Lift: Hammer Blow must never exceed static wheel load $W$:
$$B_{\text{rec}} \cdot \omega^2 \cdot b \le W$$
